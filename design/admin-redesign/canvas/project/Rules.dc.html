4

Filters

The toolbar goes, in order: search, filter buttons, then Reset, with the count on the right. Each filter is a dashed “+ Name” button. Its list always has a search box, counts, and Clear filter. Picking a filter shows its value in the button.

Where records move through stages, the stage is tabs with counts above the toolbar, not a filter.

On phone: with one filter, the button is named after it and shows the value (“Tag: vip”). With more filters, a single Filters button shows how many are set, and opens one drawer.

  • AlignedExpenses, Attributes and Promotions: their filter lists got a search box.
  • AlignedProducts: the Status filter became tabs (All · Active · Draft · Archived), like Promotions.
  • Already followsCustomers, Collections, Categories.

5

Lists and row actions

Clicking anywhere on a row opens the record, either its page or its side panel. Controls inside a row (checkbox, tag, receipt) sit above that. A ⋯ button ends each row that has more actions. Its menu goes: open or edit, other actions, a separator, then the destructive action in red. On phone the same menu opens as a drawer.

Selecting rows shows the action bar at the bottom: how many, the actions, then Clear. When the list has pages, the bar sits in the page row, between the count and the page buttons, and that row stays at the bottom while you scroll, so the bar never covers the page buttons. On phone the bar sits above the tab bar.

  • AlignedExpenses (phone): rows got ⋯ with Edit, View the receipt and Delete. Expense category rows now open their panel.
  • AlignedProducts: the action bar no longer covers the page buttons; it sits in the page row.
  • Already followsEvery other list.

7

Saving and deleting

Full pages show a save bar once something changes: Discard · Save, or Cancel · Create … for a new record. Delete lives in the header ⋯.

Panels and dialogs end with Cancel · Save, with Save on the right. On desktop, Delete sits at the left of that footer. On phone, it’s a red text button at the end of the form.

Every delete asks first in an alert. Typing a word to confirm is only for deletes that reach many records or personal data (an attribute in use, deleting a customer’s data).

Button words: Save when editing. When creating, the verb and noun from the app’s own text: Create promotion, Add customer, Record expense.

  • AlignedCategories (phone): the bordered “Delete category” block became the plain red button used elsewhere.
  • AlignedCustomer, address and expense panels: Delete sits at the footer’s left (Archive for expense categories). New expenses save with “Record expense”.

Messages

Field errorRed text under the field once they try to save. With several, a red alert at the top also says how many.
NoteGrey box with an icon, for neutral information (“Each one posts itself…”).
AlertRed outlined box, only for problems and blockers.
ToastBottom right (above the tab bar on phone), for something that’s done.
EmptyIn a list: centered grey text and at most one button. In a form section: a dashed box with one sentence.
LoadingThe page’s own frame (title, toolbar, column headings) right away, with grey placeholder rows or cards where the data goes. No spinner over the page.
Failed to loadIn place of the rows or cards: what didn’t load, one sentence on why if known, and Try again. The rest of the page keeps working.
  • AlignedEvery filtered list now offers one button when it comes up empty (Reset filters, Clear search, or Record expense). The phone expenses empty state lost its dashed box.
  • AddedLoading and Failed to load, drawn once on the “Loading and errors” boards for every list and record page to follow.

11

One layout, and dates

Every page has the same header (search, Create, View store, বাংলা | English, theme, Help, account) and the same sidebar. There are no section tabs: the sidebar lists each area’s pages, and on phone the Menu tab does. Plan and billing lives in Settings only.

Dates put the day first: 29 Oct, 29 Oct 2026 with the year, 29 Oct, 4:05 PM with a time.

  • AlignedEvery board took the Products board’s header and sidebar. Orders, Catalog, Inventory and Reports lost their section tabs. Billing left the sidebar.
  • AlignedDates written month first (Oct 29) now put the day first.
